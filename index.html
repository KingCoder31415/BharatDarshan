Culture of Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh is a state rich in cultural diversity, boasting a variety of traditional art forms, classical dances, and festivals that are celebrated with great enthusiasm. The state is known for its Kuchipudi dance form, which is one of the eight classical dances of India. Traditional arts such as Kalamkari painting and Bidriware, along with its classical music traditions, add to the rich cultural heritage.

Festivals

From Pongal to Sankranti, Andhra Pradesh celebrates a wide range of festivals with vibrant displays of culture, music, and dance.

Art & Craft

The state is home to exquisite arts like Kalamkari (hand-painted or block-printed cotton textiles) and Bidriware (a metal craft known for intricate designs).

Dance & Music

Kuchipudi, the classical dance of Andhra Pradesh, along with Carnatic music, forms an integral part of the cultural fabric.
